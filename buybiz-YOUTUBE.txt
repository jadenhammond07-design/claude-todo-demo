DESCRIPTION
What if you didn't have to invent the next big company to become a business owner? Plenty of ordinary people buy a business that already works. In this video: how buying a business works, why existing customers are worth paying for, revenue vs profit vs cash flow vs debt (with a worked laundromat example), why owners sell, and the biggest trap first-time buyers fall into.

All numbers are examples. Example deal: $270,000 price, 10% down, $243,000 borrowed at about 10.5% over 10 years is roughly $3,280/month (~$39,000/year), leaving about $51,000 of $90,000 cash flow before taxes. Real prices, rates and loan terms vary.
This video is for education, not financial or legal advice. Get an accountant and a lawyer before buying a business.
